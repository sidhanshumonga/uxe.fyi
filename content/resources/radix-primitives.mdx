Study the source, not just the docs. Focus management, dismissable layers, typeahead and roving tab index are all solved here, and each one is a lesson in how much work a "simple" dropdown actually is.

The component APIs are just as instructive. Compound components and controlled or uncontrolled props show how to expose flexibility without leaking complexity to the consumer.

## Who it’s for

- Anyone building a design system
- Engineers who want to understand accessible patterns deeply
