This is the fastest way I know for an engineer to stop shipping interfaces that look like admin panels.

Every tip is small, concrete and shown as a before and after: reduce contrast on secondary text instead of shrinking it, use fewer borders, give spacing a scale. None of it requires talent, just applying rules consistently.

## Limits

It is about visual polish, not interaction. Pair it with something like [About Face](/resources/about-face) for behaviour.

## Who it’s for

- Engineers starting to care about how things look
- Solo builders without a designer
