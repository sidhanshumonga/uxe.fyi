Read it for the vocabulary. Signifiers, mappings, feedback, constraints, conceptual models: you will use these words in design reviews for the rest of your career.

The examples are doors, stoves and light switches rather than software, which turns out to be a strength. Physical objects make the principles obvious, and transferring them to interfaces is a useful exercise in itself.

## Who it’s for

- Everyone, early
- Engineers moving towards UX
