Each post takes one component, such as a toggle button, a menu or a card, and builds it properly from the markup up, explaining every decision along the way.

It is the best accessibility resource I know that reads like engineering rather than compliance. You come away understanding why a pattern works, not just which attributes to add.

## Who it’s for

- Frontend engineers at any level
- Designers specifying interactive components
