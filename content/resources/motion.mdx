Layout animations and spring physics without writing your own FLIP code. For most product animation needs, it removes a week of fiddly work.

My advice is to learn what it does under the hood first: how FLIP measures and inverts layout, how springs differ from duration-based easing. Then let the library save you the time, and you will know when it is the wrong tool.

## Who it’s for

- React teams adding motion to a product
- Prototypers who want realistic physics quickly
