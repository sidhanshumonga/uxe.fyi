About Face is a long book, and it reads like one. It is also the most complete account of interaction design I have found, and the one I return to when I need to explain a decision rather than just make it.

The chapters on goal-directed design reframed how I write component specs: start from what the user is trying to accomplish, then derive the states. The sections on modeless feedback and avoiding unnecessary confirmation dialogs map directly onto everyday frontend work.

## Who it’s for

- Engineers who want vocabulary for design reviews
- Designers who want the reasoning behind patterns
- Anyone writing a design system’s guidelines

Skip around. Nobody needs to read it cover to cover, and the index is good.
