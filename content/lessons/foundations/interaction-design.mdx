Every interactive element has more states than the mockup shows. Interaction design is the work of deciding what each one looks like and how the element moves between them.

## The full state list

- Default, hover, focus, active, disabled
- Loading, success, error
- Empty, partial, overflow
- Offline and retrying

## Transitions

A transition explains a change of state. It should be short enough not to delay the user, typically 120–250ms, and the direction of movement should match the spatial model: things that came from the right leave to the right.

## Feedback and recovery

Every action needs a response within about 100ms, even if it is only a pressed state; that is roughly the limit for a response to feel instant ([Nielsen](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/response-times-3-important-limits/)). Every error needs a way back. Prefer [undo over confirmation](/breakdowns/undo-systems) for anything that can be reversed.

> The happy path is designed once. The other states are where users actually spend their time.
