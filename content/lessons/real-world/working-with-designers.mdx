The best UX Engineers make designers’ work better rather than just implementing it. That starts with how you show up to the collaboration.

## Get in early

Join exploration, not just handoff. A five-minute comment about what is cheap and what is expensive to build saves a week of rework later.

## Prototype in the real medium

Static mockups cannot show timing, scroll, or real data. When a design question depends on feel, build a quick coded prototype and review that instead.

## Kicking specs back

If a spec is missing states, say so with a list, not a complaint. If you think something will not work, show why with a prototype. Disagreement is cheaper in code than in meetings.

> Ask "what should this feel like?" before "what are the exact values?"
