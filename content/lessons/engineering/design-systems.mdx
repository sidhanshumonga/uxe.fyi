A design system is a product whose users are other designers and engineers. Its job is to make the right thing the easy thing: consistent, accessible, fast to build with.

## Layers

- **Tokens** — named decisions: colours, spacing, type, radii, motion. See [Systems thinking](/learning/systems-thinking).
- **Primitives** — unstyled or lightly styled building blocks with the hard behaviour solved: focus, keyboard support, dismissal, positioning. Libraries like [Radix Primitives](/resources/radix-primitives) are a good reference.
- **Components** — primitives with your brand applied: Button, Dialog, Select.
- **Patterns** — documented combinations for recurring problems: forms, empty states, destructive actions.

## Accessibility lives in the primitives

If the Dialog primitive traps and restores focus correctly, every dialog in every product does too. That's the strongest argument for a system: fix accessibility once, ship it everywhere.

## API design

Good component APIs are small and hard to misuse. Prefer a few named variants over many booleans, compose with children instead of passing render props for everything, and support both controlled and uncontrolled use where state is involved.

## Governance

Systems decay when nobody decides what gets in or out. Define how teams propose a new component, how breaking changes are versioned and announced, and how old components are deprecated and removed. Document usage with real examples and the reasons behind each decision, not just a props table.
